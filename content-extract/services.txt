Dermatology Services
The Dermatology department at
Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre
provides expert care for all your skin, hair, and nail concerns.
Our specialist is adept at diagnosing and treating common to complex skin conditions, ensuring optimal dermatological health.
Services include the management of persistent conditions such as
Acne, Scars, Pigmentation, and Melasma
We offer a range of advanced cosmetic procedures available in-house, including Dermatological Peels, Wart/Mole removal, Microdermabrasion (Diamond Peel), and treatments for Hair fall. Day-Care Services
Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre
provides a dedicated Day-Care facility for managing acute medical needs without requiring an overnight hospital stay.
This service is designed for effective treatment and close observation of acute conditions.
We offer management for acute ailments such as severe fever (including Dengue),
acute gastroenteritis, hyperemesis, asthma exacerbations, acute migraines, and dehydration. Our facility is equipped to administer crucial treatments, including IV Iron Transfusions and Vitamin Infusions/Vitality Drips. This convenient service ensures you receive high-quality, continuous medical care and IV medication in a controlled, comfortable environment, facilitating a quicker return to wellness.
