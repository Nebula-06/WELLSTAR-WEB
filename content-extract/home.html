Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre: Your Multi-Specialty Health Hub

Finding comprehensive, expert healthcare just got easier. Welcome to Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre, your premier destination for multi-specialty medical care. We understand the importance of having trusted doctors for your family’s health, which is why we offer a team of highly-skilled specialists including a General Physician, Gynecologist, Orthopedician, and Dermatologist, all under one roof. At Wellstar, our commitment is to provide personalised and compassionate treatment for every patient who walks through our doors. Whether you need routine check-ups, specialised diagnostics, or ongoing disease management, you can rely on the dedicated professionals at Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre to prioritise your well-being.

Read more

Our expert General Physicians are the first point of contact for your everyday health needs, managing acute illnesses and chronic conditions with a holistic approach. For women’s health, our experienced Gynecologists offer comprehensive care from adolescence through menopause, including pre-natal and post-natal services, fertility issues, and managing conditions like PCOS/PCOD. Furthermore, the specialised Orthopedician at Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre is ready to address all your musculoskeletal concerns, from sports injuries and fractures to chronic joint pain and arthritis, helping you regain mobility and improve your quality of life. For concerns relating to your skin, hair, and nails, our qualified Dermatologist provides advanced diagnostic and treatment services for a wide range of conditions, ensuring your skin health is always at its best. Booking an appointment to see any of these specialists is straightforward through the Apollo 24|7 platform, reflecting our dedication to patient-centric service.

The convenience of accessing multi-specialty consultations and advanced diagnostic services makes Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre the smart choice for local healthcare. We pride ourselves on creating a comfortable and welcoming environment where you can discuss your health concerns openly and receive clear, evidence-based treatment plans. The seamless integration with Apollo 24|7 allows for easy scheduling of in-clinic visits or even virtual consultations from the comfort of your home, catering to your busy lifestyle. Choose Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre for comprehensive and compassionate care from specialists you can trust.
