Meet Our Specialists. Trusted Expertise.
Dr. Shakeeb Ahmer
MBBS, DNB (Internal Medicine), MRCP (UK)
Consultant Physician & Internal Medicine Specialist
Dr. Shakeeb Ahmer is a trusted Internal Medicine Specialist with over a decade of clinical experience in adult medical care. After completing his MBBS, he earned his DNB in Internal Medicine and the prestigious MRCP (UK), building a strong foundation in evidence-based clinical practice. He has trained and served at some of India’s leading hospitals — including Narayana Hrudayalaya (Gurugram), Manipal Hospital (Hebbal, Bangalore), St. Philomena’s Hospital, and Apex Hospital — gaining extensive experience in diagnosing and managing complex, multisystem disorders.
Dr. Shakeeb’s practice covers the full spectrum of adult healthcare, from preventive check-ups and lifestyle counselling to the management of chronic conditions such as hypertension, thyroid disorders, and infectious diseases. He has a special interest in Diabetes Mellitus, focusing on helping patients achieve optimal sugar control and prevent long-term complications through personalised, evidence-based care. Known for his thorough approach, Dr. Shakeeb believes in listening carefully, taking detailed histories, and arriving at an accurate diagnosis before starting treatment. His commitment is simple yet powerful  to provide clear, effective, and compassionate medical care, guided by the latest international standards.
At Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre, Dr. Shakeeb Ahmer works alongside a multidisciplinary team to ensure every patient receives comprehensive,and continuous care, with expertise, empathy, and integrity at every stage of life.
Dr. Mehnaz Rashid
MBBS, DNB (Obstetrics & Gynecology), FMAS, DMAS, ICRS (Robotic Surgery)
Consultant Obstetrician & Gynecologist
Dr. Mehnaz Rashid is a highly skilled and compassionate Obstetrician & Gynecologist, specialising in high-risk pregnancy management, minimally invasive gynecologic surgery, and reproductive health care. After completing her MBBS, she pursued a DNB in Obstetrics & Gynecology, followed by a Fellowship and Doctorate in Minimal Access Surgery (FMAS & DMAS), and ICRS certified Robotic Surgery training, equipping her with advanced surgical expertise for safer, faster, and precise outcomes. She has served at leading institutions including the High-Risk Pregnancy Unit at Bhagwan Mahaveer Jain Hospital, Koshy’s Hospital, and is a Visiting Consultant at Cloudnine Hospital, while currently practicing as a Consultant at Motherhood Hospital.
Dr. Mehnaz is deeply committed to volunteer and philanthropic work, conducting women’s health camps at Al Ameen Hospital and worked at  JMJ Hospital, providing comprehensive consultations and surgeries for underprivileged women. Her approach to care is empathetic, evidence-based, and holistic, integrating nutrition , exercise therapy and everyday herbs into personalised treatment plans bridging modern medicine and nutritional wellness for optimal health outcomes. A passionate women’s health advocate, she addresses gender disparities in medical science, raises awareness about maternal and reproductive health, and emphasises women’s education to empower informed health decisions.
At Wellstar Polyclinic & Diagnostic Centre, Dr. Mehnaz leads the Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology, offering women comprehensive, personalised, and continuous care, with expertise, empathy, and integrity at every stage of life.
